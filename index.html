What's happening

  • Farmers cut wheat in the fields and haul it to the produce stall for coin.
  • The baker buys grain at the stall, bakes at the shop (watch the chimney), then sells bread.
  • Villagers fetch water from the well and shop for bread.
  • Travellers arrive on the roads, trade in the square, rest at the inn, and leave by another road.

Every sprite is generated in code as pixel art at runtime — no image files. The look controls above re-bake the whole town, which makes this a fast way to judge a style.
